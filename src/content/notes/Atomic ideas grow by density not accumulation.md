---
title: Atomic ideas grow by density not accumulation
type: note
stage: seedling
last_tended: 2026-02-28
---
A note does not grow by accumulating more ideas, it grows by being developed. An evergreen note is well connected. It will not always grow in length, it may grow in connections and deepness. This works best when each note only gathers one idea because [[Atomic notes can be read without context]].
