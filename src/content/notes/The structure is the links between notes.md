---
title: The structure is the links between notes
type: note
stage: seedling
last_tended: 2026-02-28
---
The primary organising principle in a digital garden is the connection between notes, rather than when they were written chronologically.
The structure of the garden is the links between digital notes, not the folder structure or the index pages.
The file structure is less important than how well the notes are connected to each other.
